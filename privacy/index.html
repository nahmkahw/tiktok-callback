Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Namkhao Publisher is a private tool operated by the owner of the "น้ำข้าว AI & Wealth" page. It is used only by its owner to send short videos that the owner created to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name) to confirm which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to upload drafts to the connected account.

How data is used and stored

  • Tokens are stored on the owner's private server and are never shared, sold, or used for any other purpose.
  • The tool does not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers, followers, or their content.
  • Videos are uploaded only to the owner's account inbox; nothing is published without the owner completing the post in TikTok.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.
